# Bibliographic References and Resources – Arechi Urban Study

This document compiles the bibliography, regulatory references, and technical documentation used in the development of the urban study for the Arechi Stadium area and Gipo Viani Square in Salerno.

## 1. Local Urban Planning Instruments

- **Municipality of Salerno – Municipal Urban Plan (PUC):**
  - *Description:* Technical implementation rules and zoning tables for the San Leonardo / Arechi area.
  - *Reference:* Framework for urban transformation and redevelopment for sports facilities and interchange parking areas.

- **Region of Campania – Regional Geoportal:**
  - *Resource:* Regional topographic database, high‑resolution ortho‑photos (2020/2022) and a 5 × 5 m DTM for altitude extraction.

## 2. Scientific Literature

- **Mitigation of Urban Heat Islands (UHI):**
  - *Article:* Santamouris, M. (2014). *Cooling the cities – a review of reflective and green roof mitigation technologies to fight heat island and improve comfort in urban environments*. Solar Energy, 103, 682‑703.
  - *Application:* Choice of tree species and high‑albedo/permeable paving materials for the Viani Square.

- **Integration of Photovoltaics into Parking Infrastructure (Solar Carports):**
  - *Article:* McKuin, B., et al. (2021). *Solar carports: A review of technology, benefits, and financial feasibility in urban settings*. Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews.
  - *Application:* Preliminary sizing of photovoltaic canopies over the stadium parking.

- **Mobility Planning for Large Events and Pedestrian Flows:**
  - *Article:* Helbing, D., & Al‑Abideen, H. Z. (2012). *Pedestrian crowd dynamics and congestion management at major sports events*. Transportation Research Procedia.
  - *Application:* Modelling of metro‑to‑stadium routing based on walkability graphs.
